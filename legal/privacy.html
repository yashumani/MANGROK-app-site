Draft for counsel review

Privacy notice

Last updated: August 10, 2026. This draft describes the implemented product architecture and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.

Device-only mode

Recipes, sealed ciphertext, settings, and attached binary files are stored in the user's browser. Mangrok's operator does not receive this data merely because the public site is opened. Clearing browser storage can delete it. JSON backups omit binary attachments.

Cloud mode

After an operator activates Supabase and a user signs in, account identifiers, recipes, encrypted sealed-note fields, permissions, media metadata, private files, activity, books, print requests, and legacy instructions may be processed to provide the service. Sealed-note passphrases are not intentionally collected or stored.

Sharing

Users choose recipients and permissions. A recipient can copy anything visible to them. Link tokens may expire, be view-limited, or be revoked. Optional secret material remains encrypted with a separate key placed after the URL fragment.

Printing and legacy

Physical printing may require a private PDF, shipping details, payment, and a print provider. Legacy instructions require human identity and eligibility review and are never released solely by a timer.

Retention and rights

The operator must publish final retention periods, subprocessors, legal bases, regional rights, contact details, and deletion procedures before production cloud activation. The application supports export and deletion requests, but an operator must complete cloud deletion after the cooling-off period.

Security

See the repository security policy and threat model. No system can guarantee that visible content will not be copied or that a compromised device will protect plaintext.

# Image asset policy

Mangrok uses original project-owned recipe illustrations and generated culinary compositions. Design research from Dribbble or other galleries is used only to study layout, hierarchy, color, spacing, and interaction patterns.

Do not commit:

- copied Dribbble shot imagery;
- paid template source files;
- marketplace vectors without a compatible project license;
- third-party stock images without recorded license and attribution requirements;
- external image hotlinks that expose user activity or can disappear without notice.

Every production asset should record its origin, license, creator or generator, creation date, alt text, and intended usage. Decorative images must not be used as proof of physical recipe testing or food-safety compliance.

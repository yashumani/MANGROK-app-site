# Legacy safety

Mangrok records instructions; it does not claim to be a will, probate service, or automatic executor.

The scheduled function may flag a plan when an earliest date or inactivity threshold is reached. It must never contact recipients, decrypt secrets, or release recipes. A trained reviewer must verify the owner's instruction, current account status, applicable evidence, recipient identity, disputes, and legal requirements. Every decision needs an immutable audit record and a second-person review for high-risk releases.

Owners must be able to cancel or update plans. Recipients need a secure claim process. Ambiguous, contested, or unverifiable plans remain blocked. Legal counsel must approve jurisdiction-specific terms before the feature is marketed as a legacy service.
